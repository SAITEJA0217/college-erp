# MRUH ERP - Head of Department (HOD) Manual

## Overview
The HOD Dashboard provides high-level oversight of a specific academic department. HODs can monitor faculty performance, student metrics, and manage departmental schedules.

## 1. Department Monitoring
- **Department Overview**: View aggregate statistics for your department.
- **Faculty Directory**: Access details of all faculty members assigned to your department, including their contact information and current class load.
- **Student Roster**: View all students enrolled in programs under your department.

## 2. Timetable & Announcements
- **Department Timetable**: View the overarching schedule for all sections within your department to ensure no faculty conflicts exist.
- **Announcements**: Broadcast important notices directly to students or faculty within your specific department without needing Admin intervention.

## 3. Reports
- Generate academic and attendance reports to identify struggling students or sections with low attendance.
- Review internal marks uploaded by faculty before finalization.

## 4. Support
- **Help Desk**: Address escalated tickets or departmental inquiries.
