# MRUH ERP - Student User Manual

## Overview
The Student Dashboard is your central hub for all academic activities, from checking timetables to submitting assignments and viewing final results.

## 1. Academics & Dashboard
- **Dashboard**: Displays a quick overview of your today's classes, recent announcements, and overall attendance percentage.
- **Timetable**: View your weekly schedule.
- **Attendance**: Check your subject-wise attendance breakdown to ensure you meet the required threshold.

## 2. E-Learning
- **Study Materials**: Download notes, PDFs, and reference materials uploaded by your professors.
- **Assignments**: View pending assignments, their deadlines, and submit your work directly through the portal by uploading files.
- **Quizzes**: Take online quizzes created by your faculty. Ensure you submit before the timer runs out. Your score is available instantly upon completion.

## 3. Results & Placements
- **Internal Marks**: View your continuous assessment scores throughout the semester.
- **Results**: Check your final End-Semester grades, SGPA, and CGPA.
- **Placements**: View upcoming campus drives, company criteria, and track your application status.

## 4. Support
- **Help Desk**: Having trouble with a grade mismatch or a portal bug? Open a ticket, and an administrator will assist you.
- **Profile**: Keep your contact details up to date and change your password securely.
